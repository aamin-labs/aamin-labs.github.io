# Hermes Google Workspace

A personal AI assistant integration maintained by **aamin-labs**, for use with the owner's own Google account.

Hermes runs on the owner's computer and uses Google account permissions to carry out requested tasks. Relevant Google data is sent to OpenAI for AI processing. This is a personal project, not a service accepting public registrations.

[Privacy policy](./privacy.html)

For project questions, [contact the maintainer on GitHub](https://github.com/aamin-labs/aamin-labs.github.io/issues). Do not post private Google data or credentials in public issues.
