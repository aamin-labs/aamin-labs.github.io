# Privacy policy

Updated: 7 September 2026

## Personal use
Hermes Google Workspace is a personal integration maintained by aamin-labs. It runs on the owner's own computer and connects only to the owner's Google account. It is not offered as a public service.

## Google data and its use
Hermes accesses Google account data through permissions granted during Google authorization. The data accessed depends on the permissions granted and the tasks the owner requests. Google data is used to perform those personal assistant tasks.

## OpenAI processing
Relevant Google data is sent to OpenAI to process the owner's requests and generate responses. This means processing is not entirely local. OpenAI's applicable service terms and the owner's OpenAI account settings govern its handling and retention of that data.

## Storage and deletion
Hermes runs on the owner's computer. Local credentials and any saved conversations, logs, or downloaded data are controlled through that installation. This policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. The owner can remove locally retained data and credentials and manage data held by OpenAI through the applicable OpenAI service controls.

## Control of access
The owner can revoke Hermes access in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete data already stored locally or sent to OpenAI.

## This website and contact
This public informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not provide a Google sign-in form or request Google account permissions.

For questions, [contact the maintainer on GitHub](https://github.com/aamin-labs/aamin-labs.github.io/issues). Do not include private account data or credentials in public issues.
